How often should you professionally clean carpeting?

Most carpet manufacturers suggest a professional cleaning every 12 to 18 months. Every home is different, but we recommend at least once a year.

How much does it cost to clean a room?

Carpet is priced by square footage, with a minimum charge. Upholstery and leather are priced per piece and stairs per stair. Weekends, after hours and extra services cost more. Call for your free written estimate.

Do I need to move anything before you arrive?

Furniture moving is an optional extra service. Please take any breakables off the top of, or out of, the furniture you want moved.

Do I need to vacuum before you clean?

A pre-vacuum is highly recommended. We also offer a pre-vac service, which may add to the cost.

Do I need to be home while you clean?

No. Just give us entry details, a clear description of the work and a cell number if you'd like the price first. Payment is due at the time of service unless arranged ahead, and pets need to be secured.

When can we walk on the carpet after it has been cleaned?

Right away. Socks or clean shoes are best, and take care stepping from damp carpet onto tile or wood. Wiping your dog's paws helps prevent soil spots while it dries.

How soon can we put furniture back onto the clean carpet?

You can put furniture back while the carpet is damp if you protect the legs with foil, plastic wrap or wax paper, or ask us for pads or blocks. Usually it can go back the next day without protectors.

What can we use ourselves to spot clean?

Go easy on soap-based products, which can set stains or leave a residue that attracts dirt. Club soda works well on spills, and vinegar and water helps with some spots like wine or coffee.
